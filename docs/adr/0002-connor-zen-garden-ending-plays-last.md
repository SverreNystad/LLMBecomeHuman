# Connor's Zen Garden ending plays last, as its own Chapter Segment

In the game, Connor's deviant ending (Hart Plaza after the battle, then Amanda trapping him in the Zen Garden) happens during Markus's victory speech. Before this change, that ending was part of Connor's 32.4, which played before Markus's own final Chapter Segment (32.5/32.6), where Markus can still die. So Connor's ending branched on a Fate that could still change. Adding the emergency-exit choice made that worse: giving up there means Connor shoots Markus. We split it out: everything up to the CyberLife Tower stays in 32.4, and the Hart Plaza aftermath and Zen Garden become 32.7, Connor's last Chapter Segment, played after Markus's. It now branches on Markus's final Fate. A reader seeing a Connor segment after Markus's ending should know this is deliberate, not a routing mistake.

## Consequences

- Runs recorded before this change keep their old routes. Their Connor ending sits inside 32.4, and no other Run can reach it there.
- Connor shooting Markus in 32.7 sets Markus's Fate to dead after Markus's own ending has played. No later scene reads that Fate.
